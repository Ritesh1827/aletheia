How to read a verdict

Three rules. No fine print.

/ 01

Verified means consistent

Available history checks passed. It does not independently prove the physical item is genuine.

/ 02

Conflicts show evidence

Critical verdicts always name the rules, reference the events, and state the next step.

/ 03

Failures are honest

An unavailable service is an error with a retry — never an empty all-clear.
